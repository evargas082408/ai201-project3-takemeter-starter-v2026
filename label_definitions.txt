analysis: makes a point about basketball that rests on a specific fact you could look up (a stat, a score or record, a contract figure, a date, a draft pick, a rule, or a named play or matchup), so that taking the fact out makes the point fall apart
hot_take: a confident claim about a player, team, coach or the league, predictions included, with nothing checkable holding it up
reaction: the writer responding to something that just happened (a game, a play, an injury, a trade, a ruling) with how it made them feel, not with a claim that reaches past it
